Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to Hangul Out: Read Real Korean (the "Application"), a mobile app for Android made by Tara Soft (the "Service Provider").

Summary

  • There are no user accounts. The Service Provider does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal details.
  • Your learning progress stays on your device.
  • The Application shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect some data from your device to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • The Application offers one optional in-app purchase (removing ads), handled by Google Play.

Information kept on your device

Your learning progress and settings (such as stars earned, words reviewed, your chosen voice and whether the daily reminder is on) are saved only on your own device. They are never sent to the Service Provider or to anyone else, and they are deleted when you uninstall the Application.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Application shows banner ads and occasional full-screen ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure how they perform and prevent ad fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and sends to Google:

  • your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate its general location;
  • interactions with the Application and its ads (for example app launches, taps and ad views);
  • diagnostic information about the Application's and the SDK's performance;
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.

This data is encrypted in transit. It is collected and used by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. The Service Provider does not receive this data in a form that identifies you.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

In-app purchase

You can buy "Remove ads" inside the Application. The purchase is processed by Google Play; the Service Provider never sees your payment details. The Application only checks with Google Play whether the purchase was made, so that ads stay off on your device.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily review reminder, the Application schedules one notification a day on your device. The reminder is created on your device and no data is sent anywhere for it. You can turn it off at any time in the Application's settings or in your device's settings.

Voice

Korean words are read aloud from recordings included in the Application. If a recording is missing, the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device may be used instead, on your device under its own terms. The Application does not record your voice and does not use the microphone.

Children

The Application is not directed at children under 13, and the Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

This privacy policy is effective as of 2026-11-24.
