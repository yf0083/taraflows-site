Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to Hangul Out: Read Real Korean (the "Application"), a mobile app for Android made by Tara Soft (the "Service Provider").

Information collection and use

The Application does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not need an internet connection.

Your learning progress (such as stars earned and settings) is saved only on your own device. It is never sent to the Service Provider or to anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the Application.

Text-to-speech

To read Korean words aloud, the Application uses the text-to-speech engine that is already installed on your device. The words are handled by that engine on your device under its own terms; the Application does not send them anywhere.

Third parties

The Application does not use third-party services that collect information about you. Downloads and updates are handled by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The Application does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

This privacy policy is effective as of 2026-11-03.
